About Christine

Redefining Business Strategy with Human Intelligence

Named an industry "Disruptor" in 2019 by Utah Magazine and awarded the coveted "Utah 40 Over 40" in 2024, Christine Wzorek has redefined how Human Capital works within the framework of modern business.

With 25 years of collective business strategy and Human Capital experience, she synchronizes business constructs to incorporate human intelligence as a proactive strategy for sustainability and perpetual impact — recognizing and harnessing the latent potential within Human Capital to optimize organizational efficiencies and return value to the bottom line.

A Forbes Council writer and Chair of ACG Utah's Women's Professional Network, Christine has modernized HR for companies including Pitted Companies, DiscGenics, TORUS, Gozney, SABCA, and Level Home.
