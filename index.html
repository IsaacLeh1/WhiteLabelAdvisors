About the Firm

Trailblazers in Human Capital Strategy

White Label Advisors is a Human Capital advisory firm that marries Human Capital, Business Strategy, and Financial Strategy through the lens of human behavior — incorporating human intelligence as a proactive strategy for sustainability and perpetual impact.

We partner with CEOs, CFOs, and COOs to build People Strategy into the business development phase and ongoing strategy — not after — helping organizations assimilate to the Future of Work through higher profits, sustainable growth, and expanded market share.

Founded and led by Christine Wzorek — a recognized Human Capital industry "Disruptor" and Forbes HR Council member with 25 years of strategy experience — the firm brings big-picture vision and comprehensive process design to every engagement.
